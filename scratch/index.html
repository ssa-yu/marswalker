1 · Scratch off the orbital map

Drag over the map: the blurry orbital cover (CTX) comes off and shows sharp HiRISE detail with its terrain classes.

2 · Find places worth a walk

Uses only what you scratched open; picks up to 3 places.

How places are chosen
  1. Candidates on a 20 m lattice, only where at least 80 % of the 40 m circle around them is revealed.
  2. The spot itself is firm or rough (not sand, not blocked), with no revealed sand within 20 m.
  3. Score = terrain variety in the 60 m circle (Shannon entropy of firm / rough / sand / blocked shares, 0–1) + 0.4 per SuperCam site within 30 m that lies in the revealed area (up to 3).
  4. Must be reachable and back within the usable oxygen on your map (revealed = HiRISE classes, unrevealed = orbital prior).
  5. Highest scores first, at least 150 m apart.

About this demo

Numbers here are illustrative only and are not part of the before/after comparison. Oxygen model parameters are the pipeline's assumed values. Sand classes are heuristic, not yet reviewed.

Data: CTX and HiRISE orthomosaics (NASA/JPL/MSSS, NASA/JPL/UArizona via USGS Mars 2020 TRN), terrain classes from the team pipeline (HiRISE DTM, 2 m), SuperCam LIBS oxides (PDS Geosciences), Perseverance raw images (NASA/JPL-Caltech). Habitat at Octavia E. Butler Landing assumed.

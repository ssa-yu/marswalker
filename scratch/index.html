1 · Scratch off the orbital map

The cover is what orbit gives before any recon: blurry CTX imagery (6 m) whose terrain classes say almost everything is firm. Drag over the study area to scratch it off and reveal HiRISE detail (0.25 m) with its terrain classes.

2 · Find places worth a walk

Uses only what you scratched open. Unscratched ground stays as the orbital prior.

How targets are chosen
  1. Candidates on a 20 m lattice, only where at least 80 % of the 40 m circle around them is revealed.
  2. The spot itself is firm or rough (not sand, not blocked), with no revealed sand within 20 m.
  3. Score = terrain variety in the 60 m circle (Shannon entropy of firm / rough / sand / blocked shares, 0–1) + 0.4 per SuperCam site within 30 m that lies in the revealed area (up to 3).
  4. Must be reachable and back within the usable oxygen on your map (revealed = HiRISE classes, unrevealed = orbital prior).
  5. Highest scores first, at least 150 m apart.
